The rules on one card

A card knocks the next one over if they share:

pairthe same rank: +4 mult
stepranks one apart (6→7): +2 mult. Three steps in a row add +3 more each.
suitthe same suit: +10 chips
✕ stopnothing shared: the chain stops there, and everything after it scores nothing.

Every card that falls also adds

its value in chips: 2–10 at face value, J/Q/K 10, A 11.

Special pieces

ramp×2 mult at the moment it falls. Put it late.
Goal: reach the target in 3 tips. After each tip you draw back up to 8 cards. Why it's Balatro: same chips × mult, same "add first, multiply last" skill. You see every link before you tip, so a broken chain is always your call.
